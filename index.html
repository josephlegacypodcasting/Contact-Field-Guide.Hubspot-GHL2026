Three rules

The parts that are not negotiable

If anyone on the team reads only one section of this document, make it this one.

Always send email from the “Email Safe” list

There is a saved Smart List in Contacts called Email Safe - excluye suprimidos holding 10,558 contacts. Every email campaign is built from it, never from “All”.

Why 1,080 contacts cannot receive email: 766 bounced permanently, 293 opted out, 23 are quarantined. Sending to them burns the sending domain's reputation, and in the case of the opt-outs it is legal exposure.

Almost every custom field is frozen

They are a snapshot of HubSpot as of 18 August 2026. They do not update and never will. If a contact changes jobs, Job Title will still say the same thing two years from now.

Why HubSpot filled them through automatic enrichment and its own activity tracking. That engine no longer runs. Treat them as good for segmenting today and as history, not as permanent truth.

Segmentation lives in tags, not in fields

Behaviour, stage, brand, origin and suppression are all carried in Tags. Filtering on tags is fast; filtering on custom fields is slow and brittle.

Why They were designed this way during the migration precisely so that building an audience is one filter. The dictionary below lists all 36.

What was done

How the transfer was handled

The first attempt used GHL's native HubSpot integration. It pulled 28,508 records with 1,400 errors and created 540 custom fields mirroring HubSpot's raw schema. It was scrapped and redone as a CSV import with cleaned data.

  1. 1

    Audit of all 453 columns

    169 were completely empty. Every column was categorized and rated for real business value; HubSpot's internal plumbing and derived metrics that no other CRM can reproduce were dropped.

  2. 2

    Data cleaning

    Phones to E.164, countries to ISO-2, timezones to IANA, states to full names, emails to lowercase. 232 junk phone numbers and 575 unparseable ones were removed.

  3. 3

    Suppression consolidated

    Bounces, opt-outs, quarantines and invalid addresses sat in four disconnected fields. They were unified into tags and the DND flag.

  4. 4

    Non-leads removed

    49 internal records: the team itself, role mailboxes like hello@ and support@, and form tests. Another 141 were held back for having neither email nor phone, which GHL cannot process.

  5. 5

    Phone de-duplication

    GHL merges contacts that share a number. 79 numbers were shared, which would have collapsed 82 distinct people into one record each time. The number was kept on the fullest record and cleared on the rest, so no contact was lost.

  6. 6

    Import and correction

    The first pass failed on 207 contacts whose phones carried area codes that do not exist. Google's phone validation library was added and the file reimported. Final result: 0 errors.

Practical

Building the audiences you will actually need

Filters ready to copy in Contacts. Anything involving email starts from the Email Safe list.

Warmest contacts for an offer

Email Safe + Tag is any of: engaged-clicker, webinar-attendee

Clicked an email or attended a webinar. Roughly 1,500 people.

Customers and revenue relationships

Tag is any of: retainer-client, has-deal, lifecycle-customer

Fewer than 100 contacts, but they are the ones paying. Handle separately from everything else.

Cold-base reactivation

Email Safe + Tag is: unworked + Tag is not: email-fatigued

Sales never worked them and they are not burned out by over-sending yet.

Interest by webinar topic

Email Safe + Last Registered Event contains: "webinar name"

Declared topical intent. Last Replay Watched works the same way.

Senior marketing ICP

Marketing Role is: Marketing Leadership (Head/Director/VP) + Employment Seniority is any of: Director, VP, Executive

The sharpest cut the enriched data allows.

SMS campaign

Tag is not: no-phone, suppressed-unsubscribed

Note that email bounces do belong here, and that is deliberate. Their email is dead, their phone is not.

Caveats

Data not to trust blindly

All of this was documented during cleaning. These are not outstanding bugs; they are known limits of the source data.

DataCasesWhat is going on
Phones assumed US1,010Ten digits, no country. Assumed US because 89% of the base is US. Flagged phone-assumed-us in Data Flags.
Contacts with no name2,165Any template using a name merge field renders blank or broken. Always set a fallback.
Country (inferred)4,398Guessed from timezone. 735 resolve to Russia with no other evidence — almost certainly bot traffic.
Annual Revenue2,913Third-party enrichment in round buckets. Not verified.
Original Traffic Source8,412Says "Offline Sources", which in practice means imported or enriched. Not a real channel.
City / state pairsa handfulThe source contains cases like Miami / California. The format was fixed, the value was not — fixing it would have hidden the problem.
Shared phones82Company switchboards. The number sits on one contact per group; the rest show phone-shared-blanked.

The field that was deliberately left behind

HubSpot had Current Customer = Yes on 10,741 contacts — 91% of the database. Of those, 10,038 were simultaneously marked as Lead. It was residue from an old import, not a real status. If anyone goes looking: it was not migrated, and customer status is derived from Lifecycle Stage, Retainer and Deals Count instead.
